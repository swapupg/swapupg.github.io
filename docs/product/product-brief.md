# Model Fieldnotes

An independent modern research lab by Swapnil: “Building with AI. Sharing what holds up.”

The author owns and authorizes this public, free website. Its primary readers are developers and technical leaders building AI applications. Their journey is to discover an idea, understand its supporting evidence, explore a related tool or source, and follow further work through RSS or GitHub.

The differentiator is the connection between clear explanations, inspectable sources, and usable open-source projects. Launch includes four essays, a five-paper foundational research issue, Agent Explainer, author/editorial information, feeds, and a content publishing workflow.

The owner selected modern research-lab styling, real starter content, Codex/GitHub publishing, “By Swapnil,” and RSS/GitHub following. Use static Astro on existing GitHub Pages hosting and the purchased modelfieldnotes.com domain. Readers need no accounts. No analytics, email collection, reader backend, or new paid integrations are included. The subsequently authorized editorial workflows publish daily briefings with automated checks; research and prototype releases retain their review gates. See docs/automation/README.md.

Code, content, and history are public. Draft status hides a page from the generated website, not from the source repository. Personal billing data, credentials, and private biography details do not belong in the project. Agent Explainer remains an independently deployed public beta; its simulations are not empirical model evaluations.

Success means verified reading, project, source, sharing, feed, and navigation journeys on desktop/mobile, plus accessible pages, passing build validation, HTTPS, preserved experiment links, and a documented publishing and rollback process. No audience-growth promises or invented usage metrics.

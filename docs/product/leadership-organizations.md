## Source review

Reviewed September 21, 2026:

- Gartner, March 4, 2026 public HR survey release: source-reported July 2025 manager/HR surveys. Distinguish respondent reports and hypothetical preferences from measured organizational productivity. Its causal advice is not a randomized finding.
- Gartner, May 8, 2025 public software-engineering leadership Q&A: analyst recommendations and forecasts, not proof of a universal role transition.
- Gartner, July 1, 2025 public software-engineering trends release: anticipated emphasis on system design and platform capabilities. Forecasts do not establish a universal need for more architect roles.
- NIST AI RMF 1.0, GOVERN sections on responsibilities and oversight: voluntary framework, not measured team-performance evidence.
- HBR, “How AI Is Redefining Managerial Roles,” July–August 2025: public preview accessed; use title/link as a reading suggestion only. Full article not reviewed.
- Brynjolfsson, Li, Raymond, _Generative AI at Work_, QJE 2025, author-hosted published PDF: methods, outcomes, and scope checked. Published sample 5,172; 15% average resolutions/hour improvement. Do not mix with the earlier working-paper figures. Support setting, staggered rollout, not a universal ROI estimate.
- Dell’Acqua et al., _The Cybernetic Teammate_, Organization Science, published online June 12, 2026, publisher's open-access PDF: design, outcomes, limitations checked. Published sample 791, randomized conditions and one-day product-innovation exercise. Do not mix with the older 776-person working paper; do not infer whole-team replacement or long-term company performance.

Keep summaries brief; the bulk of the site content is an original decision framework, explicit interpretations, and labeled illustrations. Present no independent reproduction of external findings.
